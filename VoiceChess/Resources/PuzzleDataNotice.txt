Puzzle positions in mate_puzzles.json come from the Lichess puzzle database:
https://database.lichess.org/

The Lichess database exports are released under Creative Commons CC0. The app
bundles 500 puzzles each for mate in one, mate in two, and mate in
three. Puzzles are sampled from the popular, frequently-played positions rated
800 to 1600, with at most 8 pieces in the starting position, and ordered by
rating from easiest to hardest.
